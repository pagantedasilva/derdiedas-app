# App marketing and ASO

## Positioning

"Stop guessing der, die, das." Short practice that teaches the patterns behind the articles. Honest, calm, no gimmicks. Validate the exact promise with the CEO before using it publicly.

## Audience segments

1. Classroom learners (A1 to B1) wanting extra practice.
2. Movers and expats preparing for life or work in a German-speaking country.
3. Exam candidates (for example Goethe or telc levels).
4. Self-learners who like patterns and rules.

For each segment, write one paragraph: situation, pain, what they search for, which channel they use.

## App store optimization

- **Keywords**: research with the store's own autocomplete and competitor listings. Group by intent: learn der die das, German articles, German noun gender, German grammar practice. Provide a ranked list of 30 candidates and the 100-character iOS keyword field proposal.
- **Title and subtitle**: give three options each, within limits, with the main keyword in the title.
- **Screenshots**: a five-frame story. 1 the problem (guessing), 2 the practice screen, 3 the rule explanation, 4 progress, 5 privacy or offline. Give the headline for each frame to the UX Copy Expert and the layout request to the UX Designer.
- **Reviews**: ask for an honest review after a successful session, never before. Reply policy: thank, answer, offer email support; hand replies to the Support Specialist.
- Keep metadata in sync with the real features. Never claim what the app does not do.

## Content ideas (each must be verified with `german-gender-reference`)

- "Five endings that make a noun feminine" with examples.
- Weekly "noun of the week" with a memory hook.
- Common exceptions that trip learners up.
- Short video: one pattern in 30 seconds.

## Launch plan outline

1. **Pre-launch (4 weeks):** landing page with email signup, beta invites in learner communities, collect honest feedback.
2. **Launch week:** store listings live, announcement drafts for the channels approved by the board, outreach list of teachers and bloggers (drafts only).
3. **Days 8 to 30:** respond to feedback, publish one content piece per week, review keyword rankings, adjust the listing.

## Metrics to report weekly

Store impressions, product page views, conversion to install, installs by source, day-1 and day-7 retention, rating and review count, top search terms, best content piece, one recommendation.

## Approval gate

Draft everything. The board approves before anything is published, posted, emailed, or paid for. Include in each plan: channel, audience, goal metric, cost, effort, and risk.

## Never

Fake reviews, review incentives that violate store policy, misleading comparisons, unverifiable claims, collecting email addresses without a privacy notice.
