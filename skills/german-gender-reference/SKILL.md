# German noun gender reference

Gender in German is mostly not predictable, but several patterns are strong. Use the confidence labels exactly. Never present a "usually" rule as "always".

## Always true (no common exceptions)

- Diminutives **-chen** and **-lein** are neuter: das Mädchen, das Fräulein, das Büchlein.
- Nouns made from infinitives are neuter: das Essen, das Lernen.
- Nouns made from adjectives or colors (as a noun) are neuter: das Blau, das Gute.
- A compound noun takes the gender of its **last** part: die Haustür (die Tür), der Haustürschlüssel (der Schlüssel).
- Days, months, seasons are masculine: der Montag, der Januar, der Sommer.
- Compass points are masculine: der Norden, der Süden.

## Very reliable (a few exceptions)

**Feminine**
- **-ung**: die Zeitung, die Wohnung
- **-heit, -keit**: die Freiheit, die Möglichkeit
- **-schaft**: die Freundschaft
- **-ion**: die Nation, die Information
- **-tät**: die Universität
- **-ik**: die Musik, die Politik (mostly)
- **-ur**: die Kultur, die Natur (mostly)
- **-ei**: die Bäckerei
- **-in** for female people: die Lehrerin

**Masculine**
- **-ismus**: der Tourismus
- **-ling**: der Frühling, der Lehrling
- **-or**: der Motor, der Autor
- **-ant, -ast, -eur**: der Elefant, der Ingenieur
- **-er** for people who do something: der Lehrer, der Fahrer (many other -er words vary: die Butter, das Wasser)
- Male people and male animals: der Mann, der Hahn
- Precipitation and weather: der Regen, der Schnee, der Wind (exceptions exist; check)

**Neuter**
- **-ment**: das Dokument, das Instrument (exception: der Zement)
- **-um**: das Museum, das Zentrum (exception: der Konsum)
- **-ma**: das Thema, das Klima
- Young beings: das Baby, das Kind, das Kalb

## Common but with exceptions (always say "usually")

- Nouns ending in **-e** are usually feminine: die Lampe, die Katze. Exceptions include der Name, der Junge, der Käse, das Ende, das Auge.
- **-nis** is usually neuter: das Ergebnis, das Geheimnis. Exceptions: die Kenntnis, die Erlaubnis.
- **-tum** is usually neuter: das Eigentum. Exceptions: der Reichtum, der Irrtum.
- **Ge-** collectives are often neuter: das Gebäude, das Gebirge. Exceptions: der Gebrauch, der Gedanke.
- Metals are often neuter: das Gold, das Silber. Exception: der Stahl.
- Trees and flowers are often feminine: die Eiche, die Rose. Exception: der Ahorn, der Löwenzahn.

## Do not teach as rules

"Round things are masculine", "kitchen items are feminine", or any meaning-based rule not listed above. Present such words as individual vocabulary.

## Procedure

1. Look the noun up in the content source first. If the source and this file disagree, stop and report to the CEO.
2. If the noun matches a rule above, you may cite the rule with its confidence label.
3. If you are not sure, write "unverified" and ask the CEO. Do not guess.
4. Mention the plural only if it was supplied; plural forms are not covered here.

## Checklist for any published example

- Noun capitalized, umlauts correct, correct article, rule label matches the tier above.
